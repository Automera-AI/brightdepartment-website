The 7-Day Launch.

A short, fixed launch keeps scope tight and gets you a working system before second thoughts set in.

  1. Call

    Free intro call

    A few questions, one demo matched to your pain, and a straight answer on fit.

  2. Day 1

    Kickoff

    Confirm the bottleneck, the win number and the baseline. Collect approved info: services, hours, FAQs, service area.

  3. Day 2–4

    Build

    Receptionist script and knowledge, automations, calendar and CRM connections, your dashboard. Tracking first.

  4. Day 5–6

    Test & train

    Urgent calls, price questions, wrong numbers, angry callers. Then about 30 minutes with your team.

  5. Day 7

    Live

    After-hours first if you like, then full. Daily monitoring for the first week.

Then, every week.

Building is about 15% of the job. The weekly sync with your team is the other 85%, and it's why the system keeps getting better instead of drifting.

  • Your number. The win number against the baseline and target.
  • Real calls. We play two or three, one good, one weak, and ask what your best person would have said.
  • Friction. What is your team working around? What did the AI get wrong?
  • Fixes. One to three changes for next week, each tested before it ships.
  • Notes in two hours. Owners and dates logged, dashboard updated.

What we need from you.

  • About 30 to 45 minutes of your time up front.
  • Read-only access to your tools first. You approve each system one at a time.
  • One staff member for a short weekly sync.
  • One number that would make this a win, agreed in writing before we start.

Good questions.

What if AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from information you approve, hands off when it isn't sure, and every call is reviewed in the weekly sync. Each mistake becomes a test case so it doesn't happen twice.

Do you replace my office staff?

No. We take the repetitive work off their plate (answering after hours, chasing quotes, data entry) so they can do the work that needs a person.

Who owns the setup if I leave?

It's built in your own tools, so there's nothing to migrate. Month to month, no contract.

Is my data safe?

Your data is used only for your business. We use read-only access first, you approve each system one at a time, and we never ask for passwords by email or chat.
