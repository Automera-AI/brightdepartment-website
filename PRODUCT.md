## Platform

web

## Stack

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, deployed on Netlify. No framework and no build step. This is the owner's choice. The site should not feel static: it carries rich content, the hero promo video, and authored motion. Forms use Netlify Forms, with a planned webhook into GoHighLevel. Booking uses the embedded GoHighLevel calendar (https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/bookings/consultations-x8yc0), configured in assets/js/config.js. If the calendar is missing, the page falls back to a "request a time" form.

## Users

The main users are owners of local, owner-operated service businesses in Richmond and Central Virginia with roughly 5–25 staff. First targets are roofing and exteriors, HVAC and plumbing, and general contractors; automotive, retail, and real estate or property management follow. These owners live on the phone. Their crews are on jobs while it rings, web leads wait hours, and quotes go cold. They usually reach the site in one of two ways:

- **Warm:** after a cold call from Bright Department's AI caller, Joan, and a 60–90 second custom demo video sent by email. They arrive to check whether the company is real and to book a call.
- **Cold:** from Google, the Google Business Profile, Instagram (@brightdepartmentai), referrals, or partners. They arrive to work out what this is, whether it fits their trade, and what missed calls cost them.

Their job on the site is to decide whether Bright Department is worth a free call, then book it, send a question, call the AI line, or take something away (the calculator, the Scorecard, or the prompts guide).

## Product Purpose

Bright Department is a done-for-you AI department for local small businesses, based in Short Pump and serving Richmond and Central Virginia. It builds, runs and keeps improving three things inside the tools a business already uses:

- AI receptionists and call bots that answer calls and texts 24/7, follow up every lead, book jobs, and ask for reviews.
- Workflow automations, such as quote follow-up, rebooking, invoicing handoffs, review requests, and data entry.
- Company-wide dashboards. This is the core service: one main console showing calls, leads, jobs, cash, and reviews, across one business or several.

The website is a sales funnel in its own right, not a brochure. Success means a visitor at any level of readiness takes a tracked next step: they book a call, send the contact form, call the AI line, use the calculator, or download a lead magnet.

## Positioning

A local, accountable AI department that sells a measured result rather than software. Bright Department finds the owner's single biggest bottleneck using four lenses (Reach, Acquire, Convert, Expand), agrees one "win number," goes live in 7 days, and keeps tuning through weekly syncs with the client's staff.

It sells only what it already runs in its own businesses:

- **288 Exteriors**, the owner's roofing, siding and gutters company, is "client 0." The client template is built there first.
- **The owner's rental real estate portfolio** runs on the same systems.
- **Bright Department's own sales run on its own AI:** the dialer, the inbound AI receptionist, and the dashboards.

No local competitor is actually based in Short Pump, shows a live AI line, or offers dashboards.

## Operating Context

- **Demo line:** (804) 635-3682 is the only public number. An AI assistant answers it and takes a message so the team can call back. It never books or quotes.
- **Funnel stages:** list → dial → capture (decision-maker's name and email) → custom demo video → booked intro call → discovery (bottleneck and win number) → proposal within 24 hours → signed → 7-Day Launch → live.
- **Intro call:** the GoHighLevel event is a 30-minute "Free AI Automation Discovery Call". The video says 15 minutes and the demo emails say 10. The final length is still open, so the website does not state a minute count.
- **Missed-call formula:** missed calls per week × 4.33 × close rate × average job value. The calculator and discovery calls both use it.
- **Lead magnets:** the AI Growth Scorecard (about 12 questions that find the weakest stage) and "25 AI Prompts That Get Local Businesses More Customers."
- **Contact:** team@brightdepartment.com, Instagram @brightdepartmentai.
- **Service area:** Richmond, Short Pump, Glen Allen, Henrico, Chesterfield, Midlothian, Mechanicsville, and Central Virginia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Offer:** the 7-Day AI Department Launch. Live in 7 days, no contract, cancel anytime.
- **No prices on the website.** The owner decided this. Price questions get the value answer ("for most trades, one recovered job a month covers it") and a booking CTA.
- **Guarantee:** "If we don't pay for ourselves in 90 days, you get a full refund." It must appear word for word, and only after the owner approves it for public use with lawyer-reviewed terms. It is not yet approved for the website.
- **The three AI employees** in the offer are the Receptionist, Follow-up & Booking, and Marketing & Social. Reviews and reputation are a supporting service.
- **Texting:** SMS goes only to people who opt in, after A2P 10DLC registration. No business emails on weekends. Nothing is sent without the owner's approval.
- **Naming:** always write "Bright Department" in full, never abbreviated in visible text. On calls it is spoken as "Bright Department AI." Refer to staff as "our team."
- **Undecided:** the intro call length, whether to show the owner's face or name, and whether "only 5 new businesses a month" is a real capacity limit.

## Product Principles

1. Bottleneck first: lead with the owner's problem and their own numbers, never the technology.
2. Prove, don't claim: show the live line, the dashboard, and our own businesses running on it. Label samples.
3. Every visitor gets a next step that matches how ready they are, and every step is tracked.
4. Local and accountable: Short Pump-based, with real people behind it and weekly syncs.
5. Honesty over hype: no invented results, and the guarantee only in its exact approved wording.
