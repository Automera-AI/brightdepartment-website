# Surface: brightdepartment.com marketing site (all public pages)

Mode: Persuade. Scope covers home, services, how it works, case studies, industries, locations, book, contact, free tools (calculator, Scorecard, 25 AI Prompts), thank-you, privacy and terms. Every page uses one world.

- **Audience:** owner-operators of Richmond-area trades (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, GC first). They arrive warm from a demo email or cold from Google, GBP or Instagram.
- **Job:** decide whether Bright Department is worth a 10-minute call, then act.
- **Action ladder:** Book a call (main), Call (804) 635-3682 (second), contact form, then calculator, Scorecard or Prompts.
- **Proof:** 288 Exteriors as client 0, the rental portfolio, Bright Department's own AI sales, the live AI line, and a dashboard labeled SAMPLE. Case study numbers are placeholders until the owner supplies them.
- **Constraints:** no prices; the guarantee is not yet approved for the site; no invented results; brand name written in full.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the side of a good work truck: name, trade and phone number lettered big enough to read from the road. It refuses the category's dark navy, purple-gradient AI glow with icon-tile cards, which is exactly what the current site ships.

OWN-WORLD: The ground is fleet-white enamel, with cut-vinyl lettering in sign black and reflex cobalt. Safety yellow is reserved for action, and signal red only for missed calls. A cobalt and yellow fleet stripe band runs on reflective-tape diagonals. Big Shoulders Display is the sign gothic; Barlow is the highway-sign text face. Every rule and icon uses one stroke weight, and corners are squared like plate edges.

STORY: Visitors see that a local Short Pump team answers, follows up and reports for them. They believe it because it runs 288 Exteriors and our own sales, and they can call the line right now. Then they book a call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width truck-door panel. The H1 spans two lines at roughly 7vw. The trade line sits below it, then the phone number at road scale with the yellow Book a call button beside it. The felt video plays in a slanted cab window on the right, and the fleet stripe runs along the bottom edge.

FORM: Truck Door Lettering, position 6 on the grounded list, seed key c7f786fa. Signature: one continuous cobalt line traces a call from ring to booked, scrubbed on scroll, with a red branch for the missed call. Each state is named in words.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
